what we believe

Community first.

We want to build a community of listeners who show up for each other. Show up for others and we show up for you.

Listeners first

Poets perform, but the room is the heart of it. We are building a community of listeners who show up for each other.

Free to walk in

Entry is free. Come to listen, cheer and be part of the room. Poetry belongs to everyone.

Every voice gets a turn

Two places at every reading go to poets from the audience. Put a slip in the box and you might get the stage.

Poetry for a cause

Our fundraiser shows put the room to work for a beneficiary, and anyone can give.

THE SOUND OF THE NIGHT
Ten voices, one evening.
Quiet while a poet reads.
Loud when they finish.
Two names pulled from the audience box.
Everyone walks out a little lighter.
be good to each other

Our code of conduct

PoV is for everyone. We ask everyone in the room, on stage or off, to keep it that way.

1
Listen first.
Give every poet your full attention, whether they are the feature or reading for the first time.
2
Respect everyone.
Respect everyone’s agency, religion, caste, gender and belief systems, on stage and off.
3
Leave hate at the door.
No harassment, slurs or targeting of anyone in the room.
4
Ask first.
Ask before you photograph, record or approach someone.
5
Look out for each other.
If something feels wrong, tell a host. [WHO TO TELL AT THE EVENT IF SOMETHING FEELS WRONG]
The hosts may ask anyone who breaks these to leave.
