The night

Every reading is ten voices.

One feature poet, seven poets and two Spot Slots, with the audience in the room the whole way through.

Four ways to the mic.

We want to build a community of listeners who show up for each other. If you would like to perform, here is how.

1
Attend our events each month
Come to our monthly readings. Entry is free.
2
Be incredible listeners
Give every poet your full attention.
3
Encourage fellow poets
Show up and cheer them on.
4
Sign up for Spot Slots
Put your name in our audience box. If it gets picked, you get the stage.

What we look for

Each month we pick our line-up of performers based on:

Quality of writing
Authenticity
New and refreshing perspectives
Respect for everyone’s agency, religion, caste, gender, belief systems
Insatiable love for poetry
